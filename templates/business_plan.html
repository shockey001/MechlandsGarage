Business Plan

A phased strategy for building Kenya's first integrated ICE and EV workshop.

📋

Executive Summary

The business in brief.

Mechlands Garage is an integrated automotive workshop in Nairobi, Kenya, combining traditional internal combustion engine (ICE) repair with electric vehicle (EV) servicing, ICE-to-EV conversion, performance tuning, and custom engineering.

The Kenyan automotive market is at a turning point. EV registrations have grown from approximately 2,000 vehicles in 2023 to over 40,000 today, with supportive government policy including import duty exemptions and a national electric mobility framework. Yet the country's repair industry lacks the capability to service high-voltage systems, battery packs, or electric drivetrains.

Mechlands Garage captures this opportunity through a phased, capital-efficient strategy: build cash flow from ICE repair and parts sales, then layer on EV capability and high-margin engineering work as revenue allows.

📊

Market Analysis

Why this market, why this moment.

The Opportunity

Kenya's vehicle fleet remains dominated by petrol and diesel engines, creating sustained demand for repair, maintenance, and parts. Meanwhile, the EV transition is accelerating — driven by fuel cost pressures, government incentives, and growing charging infrastructure.

The gap: almost no workshops in Kenya can service both ICE and EV vehicles, and ICE-to-EV conversion is essentially absent. Mechlands Garage is positioned in this underserved space.

Competitive Landscape

The market breaks into three tiers: informal "jua kali" workshops (low price, no warranty), brand-authorized service centers (high price, brand-specific), and a handful of independent specialists. Mechlands Garage competes in the specialist tier with a broader capability set than any current player.

Target Customers

Individual car owners (ICE and EV), SME vehicle fleets, performance and customization enthusiasts, and early EV adopters seeking reliable service and conversion options.

💼

Services & Revenue Streams

Multiple income channels, phased introduction.

ICE Repair & Parts

Core cash flow business from day one. Diagnostics, repairs, servicing, and spare parts sales.

EV Diagnostics & Servicing

High-voltage system work — a capability almost no competitors have. Premium pricing.

ICE-to-EV Conversion

High-value project work. Each conversion is a substantial engagement with follow-on service.

Performance Tuning

ECU remapping, turbo upgrades, exhaust systems. High-margin, enthusiast-driven.

Customization

Interior luxury work, infotainment, off-road builds. Margin-rich, repeat-customer oriented.

Engineering & Prototyping

Custom battery design, concept builds, fleet consulting. Positions the brand as an innovator.

Financial Highlights

Conservative, revenue-driven growth.

Phase 1 revenue model: Day-to-day ICE repair and parts sales generate steady operating income. At 2–3 basic services per day, monthly revenue reaches KES 150,000 – 300,000 excluding parts margin.

Phase 2 revenue shift: As tuning and EV services come online, average transaction value rises sharply. A single conversion project can exceed the revenue of a full month of routine servicing.

Cost discipline: The business is structured to grow from revenue, not to require continuous external funding. Investment accelerates growth — it does not sustain basic operations.

⚠️

Risk Management

Realistic assessment of the challenges.

Technical risk: EV high-voltage work requires specialized training and safety protocols. Mitigation: gradual capability build, formal certification, and standardized operating procedures.

Market risk: EV adoption could grow slower than expected. Mitigation: ICE business provides baseline stability; EV is a growth layer, not a dependency.

Regulatory risk: EV conversion standards are still developing. Mitigation: active engagement with industry bodies and monitoring of NTSA updates.

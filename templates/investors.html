The Opportunity

Why this workshop, in this market, at this moment.

Kenya's electric vehicle registrations have grown from roughly 2,000 vehicles in 2023 to over 40,000 today, with government policy actively supporting the transition — including import duty exemptions and a national electric mobility framework.

Yet the country's repair industry is not ready. Almost no workshops have the capability to service high-voltage EV systems, battery packs, or motor controllers. ICE-to-EV conversion — a proven pathway in other markets — is essentially absent in Kenya.

At the same time, millions of petrol and diesel vehicles will continue to need expert repair for years. A workshop that serves both sides of this transition captures revenue today while building capability for tomorrow. Mechlands Garage is built for exactly that.

Why Retrofit Instead of Buy New?

Importing a new electric vehicle to Kenya costs millions. But what if you could get the EV driving experience for a fraction of the price?

Retrofitting removes the combustion engine and replaces it with an electric motor and battery pack. You save on fuel, drastically reduce maintenance, and drive one of the cleanest vehicles on the road—without buying a new car.

The Conversion Process

1️⃣

Feasibility Check

We inspect your vehicle's chassis, weight distribution, and condition to ensure it is a safe candidate for conversion.

2️⃣

Design & Sourcing

We select the right motor and battery size based on your daily driving needs (range) and your budget.

3️⃣

Engineering

Out with the old (engine, exhaust, fuel tank). In with the new (motor, controller, battery pack, charger).

4️⃣

Testing & Handover

Rigorous road testing, safety checks on high-voltage systems, and final handover with a charging guide.

Common Questions

Is it legal in Kenya?

Yes. Conversions are treated as vehicle modifications. We work with you to ensure the vehicle passes NTSA inspection and is properly registered as electric.

How long does it take?

A standard conversion takes between 4 to 8 weeks depending on the complexity of the vehicle and the sourcing of parts.

What happens to my old engine?

We can either buy the old engine from you (to offset costs) or dispose of it responsibly according to environmental regulations.

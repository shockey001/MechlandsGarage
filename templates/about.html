About Mechlands Garage

Building Kenya's first integrated workshop for the transition from combustion to electric.

📖

Our Story

Why Mechlands Garage exists.

Kenya's automotive landscape is at a turning point. Electric vehicles are arriving — slowly at first, then faster than most workshops are prepared for. At the same time, millions of petrol and diesel vehicles still need expert repair, tuning, and care for years to come.

Most garages can handle one side of that transition or the other. Very few can handle both. Mechlands Garage was founded to close that gap — a workshop that repairs today's vehicles with engineering rigor, while building the skills and infrastructure to convert, service, and innovate on tomorrow's electric ones.

We are not a parts-swapping shop. We are an engineering workshop that happens to fix cars.

🎯

Our Philosophy

Three principles that guide every job we take on.

🔍

Diagnose First

We identify the real problem before quoting. No guesswork, no unnecessary replacements, no surprises on the invoice.

🛠️

Engineer the Solution

📚

The Founder

The person behind Mechlands Garage.

Gachoki Ndwiga — Founder & Lead Engineer

Gachoki Ndwiga is an automotive electrician with four years of hands-on experience in vehicle electrical systems and automotive wiring. His work spans diagnostics, re-wiring, electrical fault-tracing, and system integration across petrol and diesel vehicles — the foundation on which Mechlands Garage is built.

His apprenticeship years gave him something formal training alone cannot: an intimate understanding of how Kenyan vehicles actually fail, how owners actually drive them, and how far a well-engineered repair can extend a vehicle's life. That experience is now being extended into electric vehicle systems — battery management, high-voltage safety, and drivetrain integration — positioning Mechlands Garage to serve both today's vehicles and tomorrow's.

Gachoki founded Mechlands Garage to build the kind of workshop he wished existed when he was starting out: one that diagnoses before it quotes, engineers before it replaces, and treats every customer's vehicle as if it were his own.

🛣️

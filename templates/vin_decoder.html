Where to Find Your VIN

🚗

Dashboard

Look at the base of the windshield from outside the car. The VIN is on a metal plate on the driver's side.

🚪

Door Jamb

Open the driver's door. The VIN is on a sticker on the frame near the latch.

📄

Logbook

Your vehicle registration document (logbook) lists the VIN or chassis number.

🔧

Engine Bay

On older vehicles, the VIN is often stamped on the firewall or on the engine block itself.
